# Black Sun Empire — Production Philosophy

**Knowledge source type:** `personal_observation` — no course/interview processed yet through Phase 0.5. Treat as a low-confidence placeholder until a real source is run through the Issue pipeline.

## Core Philosophy

"Dark, cinematic, technically precise." Dark atmosphere is the primary character; technical precision is in service of emotional/cinematic depth, not precision for its own sake.

## Decisions (actionable)

- Sound design favors dark filter resonance and industrial noise layers for texture, alongside a clean processing chain (precision + darkness, not lo-fi grit). `[hypothesis]`
- Arrangement: dark intros building into atmospheric, cinematic breakdowns rather than pure-energy drops. `[hypothesis]`
- Mastering target: `loudness_strategy: controlled`, ~-6 LUFS, full but weighted dark. `[hypothesis]`

## What NOT to copy

Don't use this as a template for high-energy/aggression-first tracks (see Aggressor Bunx/Noisia instead) — the core signature here is atmosphere, not impact.

The Fever, Arrakis, Scarab, Chemical.

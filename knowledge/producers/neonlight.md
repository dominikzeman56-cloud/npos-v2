# Neonlight — Production Philosophy

**Knowledge source type:** `personal_observation` — no course/interview processed yet through Phase 0.5. Treat as a low-confidence placeholder until a real source is run through the Issue pipeline.

## Core Philosophy

"Warm analog character, modern precision." Warmth and character come from analog-style saturation, layered on top of modern clarity and precision — not a lo-fi/vintage imitation.

## Decisions (actionable)

- Sound design: warm saturation applied broadly (not just on bass), FM synthesis used specifically in bass for character. `[hypothesis]`
- Mixing/mastering: warm signal chain throughout, but kept clean rather than murky; ~-5 LUFS master target. `[hypothesis]`

## What NOT to copy

Don't use as a reference when the goal is a dark/industrial or raw/aggressive signature (see Black Sun Empire / Aggressor Bunx instead).

## Reference tracks

Pressure, Glitch, Cache, Neural.

# Aggressor Bunx — Production Philosophy

**Knowledge source type:** `personal_observation` — no course/interview processed yet through Phase 0.5. Treat as a low-confidence placeholder until a real source is run through the Issue pipeline.

## Core Philosophy

"Raw energy, aggressive processing, maximum impact." Aggression is the goal, not a side effect — technique exists to serve energy, not the other way round.

## Decisions (actionable)

- Sound design leans on heavy distortion chains and aggressive filtering applied throughout the chain, not just on the bass. `[hypothesis]`
- Mixing keeps everything forward and impact-first rather than tucked/polished. `[hypothesis]`
- Mastering target: loud and full-spectrum, `loudness_strategy: crushed`, roughly -4 to -3 LUFS. `[hypothesis]`

## What NOT to copy

Don't reach for this approach when the goal is clarity or space (see Mefjus/Burr-Oak instead) — this philosophy explicitly trades finesse for impact.

## Reference tracks

Digital World, Run, Psyho, Terror.
